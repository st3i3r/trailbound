## 2. In short

- There are no accounts, and we run no servers. Your progress is saved on your device.
- The game shows ads through Google AdMob and uses Google Firebase for analytics, crash reports and remote settings. These services collect device data as described in section 4.
- Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
- We don't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location, and we never see your payment details.

## 3. Data stored on your device

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Game progress (sites, coins, items, journal, letters, Daily scores) and settings | Saving and restoring your game |
| Remove Ads status and ad pacing (when the last ad was shown) | Applying your purchase and the game's ad limits |

This data stays on your device. If Android backup is turned on, Android may back it up to your Google account and restore it on a new device; Google's terms apply to that backup. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it from the device.

## 4. Data processed by Google services in the game

| Service | Data | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Ads: Google AdMob, with the Google User Messaging Platform for consent | Advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate an approximate location), device and app information, ad interactions, your consent choices | Showing and measuring ads and preventing ad fraud. Ads are personalised only where you consent or where consent isn't required |
| Analytics: Google Analytics for Firebase | App instance ID, gameplay events (for example the day reached, items bought, ads watched), device and app information, approximate location from the IP address; the advertising ID only where consent allows | Understanding how the game is played, balancing it and measuring ads |
| Crash reports: Firebase Crashlytics | Crash and error traces, the device's state at the time, an installation ID | Finding and fixing crashes |
| Remote settings: Firebase Remote Config | An installation ID, device and app information | Changing ad limits and other settings without an app update |
| Purchases: Google Play Billing | Google handles the payment; the game receives a purchase confirmation | Delivering and restoring Remove Ads |

Gameplay events never include names, free text or contact details. We don't share data with anyone other than these providers.

## 6. Your choices

- **Ad consent:** Settings → Privacy options reopens the consent form where the law requires one. If you decline, you may still see non-personalised or limited ads.
- **US state privacy laws:** the same Privacy options entry lets you opt out of the use of your data for targeted advertising where those laws apply.
- **Advertising ID:** you can reset or delete it in your Android settings.
- **Remove Ads:** the one-time purchase turns off the full-screen ads between days. Rewarded ads stay available and are always optional.
- **Notifications:** the game asks before sending the Daily reminder or the crew notification (when a restoration stage is ready). You can turn either off in Settings or in Android settings.
